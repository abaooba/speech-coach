# Speech Coach frontend design spec

Every screen is built from this file. Angle: **drill-first**. A speaker who wants to sound more fluent runs short timed reps in spare minutes: one thumb, one glance, next rep. Numbers are big, chrome is minimal, nothing moves unless it carries information. Two agents building two screens without talking must produce siblings. If something is missing, use the closest existing token or class; never invent a parallel one or edit this file in a task.

## 1. Screens and navigation

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | `#/onboarding` | First launch only: a promise card ("No account needed. Your takes are saved on your phone, and only there."), what is measured, where the recording and transcript go (the seven-sentence privacy paragraph, word for word, folded in `details.onboarding__privacy-details`, closed by default under the summary "How your audio is handled", with a link to how.html's "Your audio" section after it), one button. The lead paragraph draws the 64px app icon above itself with `::before` (`icons/icon.svg`), so the screen's DOM is unchanged. |
| Practice | `#/practice` (default) | Talk on a timed topic or read a passage aloud, record, stop. Home. |
| Results | `#/results/:id` | The Coach card (the coach's line about this take and its tips) over the speech map (the take's moments as two-line cards beside a rail that is linear in time, each card a button that shows its moment in the transcript; eight cards, then "Show all"), a strip of four stats with hints and "vs last take" lines (the last take of the same kind, Talk or Read), the rank card with its labelled rating, the highlighted transcript. |
| History | `#/history` | Sessions newest first with a status word, tap to reopen, delete with confirm. |
| Progress | `#/progress` | Metric switch, summary row, hand-rolled SVG line chart with the target band and the personal best; once a take was read aloud, a Talk \| Read tab bar above them charts each kind of take on its own. |
| Path | `#/path` | The skill map: the week and due check-back chips; the benchmark when due; a Units switch naming the four units with their levels, over the one unit picked, a winding line of level nodes with its crown and checkpoint. No rank. |
| Level | `#/level/:playId` | One play step by step: Goal, takes through Practice's level mode, the review of take 1, Result. Its back link returns to the Path, whose nav item stays active. No rank. |

**Flow.** On boot, if `app.store.getFlag("onboarded")` is false the router opens Onboarding; its single button sets the flag and goes to Practice. Practice is home: the empty hash, the left nav item, where "Record again" returns. It opens on the Talk tab with "About you" selected; the tab, the option and the topic stay as the speaker left them while the app is open. Stop uploads immediately (no submit step) in the background: Practice is back in idle at once, the banner at the top of the page (section 6) says "Analyzing your speech" over every screen, and the speaker may record another take or open any screen meanwhile. When the session is saved the router goes to `#/results/<id>` if Practice is on show in idle; otherwise the banner turns green with "See results", which opens it. A level take uploads in place instead (section 7). Results keeps an action bar stuck above the nav with "History" (`.btn--ghost`) and "Record again" (`.btn--primary`), or at the end of the page on a short or zoomed window; History rows open Results by id. The nav shows Practice · Path · Progress · History; on Results no item is active, and on a level (`#/level/<playId>`) the Path item is. The nav is hidden on Onboarding and while the active Practice is `recording` or `uploading` (a level take), so a stray thumb cannot abandon a take; the banner hides with it.

**Path** (`#/path`, `app.screens.path`; docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-loop-path.md section 9 and docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-tester-feedback-design.md section 5). Under the h1, a row of status chips: "This week: 3 of 4 days" with the flame, plain text and never a button ("This week: 5 days, goal met" from 4 days), and, when check-backs are due, a button chip that starts the first one ("Due today: 1 check-back: Fillers 2", "Due today: 3 check-backs"). Then the benchmark: one compact row while one is due, "Benchmark day" over "About 3 minutes" beside a secondary "Start" named "Start benchmark" (under 320px the Start drops under the words at full width), otherwise, after a first sitting, one line ("Next benchmark: Tue, Oct 28") that from the second sitting adds the change since the first ("Since your first: read slips 6 → 2, fillers 5.1 → 2.4 a minute"). Then the Units switch (section 6, Segmented control) over one block per unit in `app.path.order(history)` order, of which only the unit picked on the switch shows (the others carry the `hidden` property): a unit card (h2 title, subtitle, the why line, then its study on a muted line of its own, `.path-unit__source-line`, which the stylesheet labels "Source: ": the short credit, surnames and year ("Lambert, Kormos and Minn (2017)", `app.path.creditOf(why.entry).short`), is the link, and the work follows it in plain muted text) over the unit's nodes as an ordered list, winding left and right of centre, with an SVG path behind them in `--ok` along cleared stretches and `--grid` elsewhere. A level node is cleared (a filled `--ok` circle with a check; a `--warn` arc over half its edge once check-back 1 passes, a full `--warn` ring, gold, "Locked in", once check-back 2 does; a small clock badge while a check-back is due), current (an `--accent` circle with the level number), locked (outlined, with a lock) or resting (outlined, with a moon, "Back tomorrow"). Beside level 3 sits the optional crown ("Sudden death, optional", "180 wpm ladder, optional"), locked until level 3 is cleared and filled `--warn` once earned; after level 5 the checkpoint flag ("Checkpoint: 2-minute new topic"). Every node is a button with an accessible name ("Fillers level 2, Spot it, cleared, check-back due") that opens an inline detail card: "Clear level 2 first." when locked; "Cleared Oct 14." then the due check-back with Start, its date, or "Locked in Oct 21." when cleared; Start for a crown or a checkpoint. The current node's card is open from the start, with its short goal and Start, and Escape closes a card and returns focus to its node. The first draw picks the unit holding the screen's one primary Start, the weakest that can start, else the first in `app.path.order`. A tap on another option shows that unit alone and gives the primary look to the Start on its next step (none when that step cannot start), with no redraw, focus move, scroll or announcement. The pick is kept while the app is open, so a level visit and its back link return to it. With no takes at all the Path still shows, every unit's level 1 current.

**Level** (`#/level/<playId>`, `app.screens.level`; the same spec, section 8). One play, step by step: a back link "Path" with a chevron-left icon to `#/path`, the h1 ("Fillers, level 3", "Fillers, level 3 check-back", "Fillers crown", "Fillers checkpoint" or "Benchmark") over the level title ("Catch it live"), and a stepper, an ordered list of `app.loop.stepsFor(play)` whose items are done (a check), current (`aria-current="step"`) or upcoming. Goal is a card with the target, large, with its unit label, the length, the example and the non-example and the unit's why line with its link (Fillers level 1 adds the speaker's top fillers, a Long pauses level with prep a "Start planning" countdown with Skip, a checkpoint the "Someone is listening (in the room)" switch); its primary button ("Record take 1", or "Read it" / "Record" on a one-take play) records through Practice's level mode (`startLevelTake`, section 2). After take 1 the Review step shows the "Take 1" feedback card (`app.loop.feedbackFor`: the number against its target, the change line, up to three moments, and on Fillers level 3 "You caught 3 of 5 live."), the level's tip card and "Review 3 moments (30 s)" ("Go to take 2" when there are no moments); then "Look at 3 moments", each moment with "Play 3 s" (one clip at a time, the button reading "Stop" while it plays) and "What happened?" chips (`aria-pressed`), Spot it first on Fillers level 2, the optional "Your fix for take 2" chips, the privacy line with a lock, "Take 1's audio stays in this phone's memory for this level only, then it is deleted. It is never saved.", and "Say it again" ("Read it again" on reads, "Read a new passage" on Pace levels 1 and 2, whose every read draws a new cold read). The Result shows "Level cleared" with the verdict, the guard lines, any jump line, the two dated check-backs and "Start level 4" / "Back to path", or the value against the target, the failed guards and "The one fix: …" with "Try take 3" while attempts remain ("This level opens again tomorrow." when none do); a check-back, crown, checkpoint or benchmark shows its own verdict. Each step change is announced through `app.ui.announce` and moves focus to the new step's heading. The screen keeps one run in memory, `{playId, baseline, material, materials, takes, choices, fix, audio}`, `materials` the material each take was said or read on, by take index, so its Review and the coach read that take's own passage (a loop's takes share the one drawn at the Goal, except that every read of a Pace ladder draws a new cold read), and releases take 1's audio (stops playback, closes the AudioContext, drops the buffer and the blob, revokes any object URL) when the Result shows, the speaker leaves the level, another play starts, or the router shows a screen other than Practice or this level. Playback decodes the blob on the first Play tap, a user gesture, with `AudioContext.decodeAudioData` and plays each clip with an `AudioBufferSourceNode` (`start(0, offset, 3)`), falling back to an `<audio>` element on an object URL; nothing plays on its own. A reload starts the level again at Goal, and without audio the Play buttons give way to "Take 1's audio is gone: it is kept only while this level is open." Neither the Path nor a level shows a rank, tier, rating or rank streak.

Routing is hash-based in `app.js`. Exactly one `.screen` has `.screen--active`; the others carry the `hidden` attribute. A route to another screen, or to another session on Results, scrolls the window to the top once the new section is active; a hash that lands on the page already shown keeps the scroll. The tab title is the screen's h1, then " · Speech Coach" ("Results · Speech Coach"); Onboarding, whose h1 is the app's name, keeps "Speech Coach".

## 2. File layout and the `app` namespace

```
frontend/
  index.html          seven <section class="screen">, <nav>, scripts in the order below,
                      one inline theme script in <head>
  styles.css          tokens, base, components, screens, one file
  app.js              creates window.app, hash router, boots on DOMContentLoaded
  ui.js               app.ui: el, renderState, clear, announce, formatClock, formatDate,
                      formatDay, icon, getTheme, applyTheme, setTheme, nextTheme
  store.js            app.store: IndexedDB sessions + flags + skill-path progress,
                      on this device only
  sheet.js            app.sheet: open(...) the bottom sheet, guideOnce(name, ...) first-visit guides
  api.js              app.api.analyze
  passages.js         app.passages: [{id, kind, title, text, targetWpm: [lo, hi], source?,
                      timeLimitSeconds?, topics?, group?}], app.passages.groups(),
                      app.passages.coldReads(), app.passages.benchmark() and
                      app.passages.retired()
  delivery.js         app.delivery: the filler rules and the delivery report (analyze, classify),
                      and the live pace estimate (livePace, countsForPaceFactor, paceFactorAfter)
  rank.js             app.rank, owner-written (T36): take scores, the rating and the tiers
  rankcard.js         app.rankCard: the rank card on Progress and Results (model, build); it
                      reads the rank only through app.rank (rank.js, owner-written, T36)
  path.js             app.path: the Loop Path's units, levels, plays and rules (measure,
                      baseline, evaluate, unlocking, check-backs, the benchmark, materials)
  loop.js             app.loop: the Level screen's pure helpers (steps, feedback, moments,
                      tap matching, clips, days in words)
  coach.js            app.coach: coachMoments(report, words), coachTakeaway(moments, summary)
  nextmove.js         app.nextMove({history, cleared, today, after}) -> {kind, label, href, why}
  language.js         app.language: canCheck(session), check(session) the grammar check, build(result)
                      isReadTake(session): the one rule for a take read aloud (rank, Progress,
                      the Results arrows, History's "Read" marker)
  tips.js             app.tips.select(report, options) -> up to two {id, text}
  pauses.js           app.pauses, human-owned (T08); agents never touch it
  screens/practice.js results.js history.js progress.js path.js level.js onboarding.js
  screens/coachcard.js app.screens.coachCard: the Coach card at the top of Results (not a screen)
  how.html            static "How we measure" page: each metric's rule, band and source
  sample-session.json one sample take, loaded by Practice's "See a sample result": its
                      timings and fillers come from a real take, its words are an everyday
                      explanation written for the sample, and `coachNotes` holds the reply
                      /api/advice gave for it, so its Results opens with the notes in place
  manifest.json  sw.js  icons/   (T10)
```

Script order: `app.js`, `ui.js`, `store.js`, `sheet.js`, `api.js`, `passages.js`, `delivery.js`, `rank.js`, `rankcard.js`, `path.js`, `loop.js`, `coach.js`, `nextmove.js`, `language.js`, `tips.js`, `pauses.js`, then the seven screens, with `screens/coachcard.js` (the Results Coach card, not a screen) right after `screens/results.js`. Classic `<script>` tags, no modules, no build, no CDN. The list is fixed by `backend/tests/test_frontend_shell.py`, so a new helper lives in the module that owns its data. A new file comes only when `docs/PLAN.md` names one, as it named `path.js`, and then joins this list, that test, `SHELL` in `sw.js` (with a `CACHE_NAME` bump) and `index.html` together. The Loop Path's scripts joined them that way (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-loop-path.md section 0). Every file is an IIFE assigning exactly one `app.<name>`; `window.app` is the only global. Each file opens with a comment saying what it owns. Transcript and user text never go through `innerHTML`; build nodes with `app.ui.el`, and SVG with `createElementNS`.

Contracts (exact names):

- `app.router.go(name, params)`, `app.router.current()`. A screen registers `app.screens[name] = { mount(root), show(params), hide() }`; `app.js` calls `mount` once at boot with its `<section id="screen-<name>" class="screen">`, then `show`/`hide` on each hash change. A module never touches DOM outside its `root`; shared data goes through `app.store` and route params.
- `app.store.save(session) -> Promise<id>`, `list()` newest first, `get(id)`, `remove(id)`, `getFlag(key)`, `setFlag(key, value)` (`undefined` deletes the flag; deleting a take in History also deletes its `coachNotes:<id>` and `languageCheck:<id>`). Session: `{id, createdAt, passageId, passageTitle, durationSeconds, metrics, transcript, topic, path, coachNotes}`; `metrics` is the backend dict unchanged, `transcript` the backend word list `[{text, start, end, confidence?}]` (`confidence`, 0 to 1, is the transcriber's own sureness of the word and is missing when the provider gave none; it only feeds the `.tok--unclear` hint, never a metric, score, tip or rank), `topic` the Talk topic on screen when the take was recorded (null without one), and `path`, absent on a plain take, a Path take's `{play, kind, take, scored, passed, stage, inRoom}`: `kind` is `"loop"`, `"checkback"`, `"crown"`, `"checkpoint"` or `"benchmark"`, `passed` is null on a take that is not scored, `stage` is 1 or 2 on a check-back and `inRoom` a boolean on a checkpoint, each null otherwise. `topic` and `path` are saved on the device only. `coachNotes`, present on the bundled sample alone, is the `/api/advice` reply shipped in sample-session.json, which Results reads as it reads the server's. Audio is never stored: `save` throws on a session carrying `blob` or `audio`. Skill-path progress: `markLevelCleared(levelId, clearedAt?) -> Promise<{levelId, clearedAt}>` records an `app.path` level as cleared (`clearedAt` an ISO time, now unless given; a level cleared again keeps its first one) and `listClearedLevels()` lists the records in the order they were cleared. They live in the `pathProgress` store that database version 2 adds; the upgrade creates only that store, so saved sessions and flags stay. While a tab running an older version holds the database open, the upgrade waits for that tab to close, and every call fails at once with "Close other Speech Coach tabs to finish updating, then try again." rather than wait (boot then opens ungated); once the tab closes, the upgrade finishes and later calls go through.
- `app.passages`: everyday material only, never debate (the owner's rule of 2026-10-14): About you, Impromptu (everyday opinion questions), Interview answer, Explain something and Tell a story on the Talk tab; six explainers, six short stories, six excerpts of historical speeches and six clarity drills on the Read tab. `kind: "passage"` is read word for word and lists on the Read tab under its `group`, `"explainer"`, `"story"`, `"speech"` or `"drill"`; `app.passages.groups()` returns a new array of `{id, label}` on each call, the Read tab's groups in order (Explainer, Story, Speech, Drill), one chip each. Read as written, with a pause at each comma and stop, no text read word for word (a Read passage, a cold read or the benchmark read) shows a filler (`frontend/tests/passages_read_as_written.test.js`): none holds a "so" right before a comma, which delivery.js would count as a habit. `kind: "prompt"` is a topic to talk on and lists on the Talk tab. Every prompt has `timeLimitSeconds`. A prompt with `topics` shows one topic at a time, drawn when Practice mounts and again on each tap of its chip or of New topic (section 6, Chips); its `text` repeats `topics[0]` so every entry has text. The `free-talk` id is titled "About you"; the id stays so saved takes still find their band. Prompts come first, because Practice opens on the first entry's tab with that entry selected. `app.passages.coldReads()` returns a new array of the 30 cold reads the skill path hands out (Restarts levels 1 to 4 and their check-backs, and every read of Pace levels 1 and 2 and the Pace crown): `kind: "passage"` entries with `coldRead: true` and a 140 to 160 wpm band, each 142 to 164 words of original everyday prose (an explainer, a short story or a news-style piece) with no digits, symbols, quotation marks or number words and none of `app.delivery.HABIT_WORDS`, so a take read as written shows no misread and no filler. They are not entries of `app.passages`, so the Read tab never lists them. `app.passages.benchmark()` returns new `{read, talk}` objects on each call, the Path's benchmark (spec section 5): the passage `benchmark-read` ("Benchmark read", 140 to 180 words of original everyday prose under the cold reads' other rules) and the prompt `benchmark-talk` ("Benchmark talk", one topic in no other prompt, 90 s), both with a 140 to 160 band. Neither is in `app.passages` or `coldReads()`, so no other take practises them. `app.passages.retired()` returns new `{id, kind, targetWpm, timeLimitSeconds?}` objects for the ids saved takes still carry after they left Practice (`congress-speech`, `pf-constructive`, `rebuttal-uniforms`, `rebuttal-homework`): only the band and time limit an old take was recorded under, with no title or text, so Results (and through it History, Progress, the rank card and the tips) and `app.path.order` read an old take as before under its own saved `passageTitle`. No tab lists them and the Path never hands one out.
- `app.delivery.analyze(words, metrics) -> report` re-scores a stored take from its words, so every saved session uses today's rules: `kinds` (`"hesitation"`, `"habit"` or null per word), `fillers` (`{index, text, kind, start, end}`, "you know" is one event), `fillerCount`, `hesitationCount`, `habitCounts`, `topHabit` (`{text, count}` or null), `speechStart` (the first word's start, null without words), `speakingSeconds`, `wordsPerMinute` (hesitation sounds are not words), `fillersPerMinute`, `pauses` (`{at, length, midSentence, long}`; `long` marks a pause that reads 5.0 s or more, `LONG_PAUSE_SECONDS`, and falls between two words), `pauseCount` and `longestPause` (the pauses the Pauses card counts: every mid-sentence pause and every long one, even after a full stop), `midSentencePauseCount`, `longestMidSentencePause`, `longPauseCount`, `cleanStretch` (`{start, end, seconds}` or null), `repeatCount` and `restarts` (`{at, text, secondCopy}` per backend repeat, `secondCopy` the indexes of the words that say the phrase again), `followedScript` (true when `metrics` has a script block: the backend found the take follows the script it was sent; false for free talk and for a take that strays from its script, which the backend scores as free talk), `misreads` (`{at, word, said, wordIndex}` per `metrics.script.misread` entry, `wordIndex` the transcript word that said it, -1 when none does) and `skips` (`{at, word}` per `metrics.script.skipped` entry); both are empty for a take without a script block. Every time in the report is in recording seconds, the clock the tips print. A session with no words falls back to the backend tallies. Results, tips and Progress read this report; `metrics` stays the backend dict unchanged.
- `app.delivery.livePace(sampleRate, {band, factor}) -> {push, read, peaks, seconds}` is the live pace while a take records (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-tester-feedback-design.md section 7; validation in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-live-pace-validation.md): syllable-nucleus peaks in the microphone's samples, after de Jong and Wempe (2009), times a words-a-peak factor. A rate under 8 kHz throws a RangeError. `push(samples)` takes each Float32Array block as it comes. `read()` returns `{wpm, cue}`: `wpm` is the trailing 15 s of speech (all of it while shorter; a silence counts only up to 1.5 s), rounded to 5, or null until 5 s of speech; `cue` is `ON_TARGET`, `SLOW_DOWN` or `SPEED_UP` against `band` with 10 wpm of slack, or null without a number or without a valid band. The first number shows its own cue; a new cue shows once the number has called for it for 3 s (`LIVE_CUE_HOLD_SECONDS`, with half a read of slack, `LIVE_CUE_HOLD_SLACK_SECONDS`, since the audio at each once-a-second read comes in whole blocks). A silence over 1.5 s keeps the last reading. `peaks()` gives the peak times in seconds from the first sample pushed, `seconds()` the audio pushed. `countsForPaceFactor(words, peaks)` is true for a take of 20 s of speech or more (`PACE_FACTOR_MIN_SECONDS`, exported; the live pace row promises 30 s on the clock while a mode has no kept factor, since a take's clock also holds the lead-in before the first word and the tail after the last), 30 words or more without hesitation sounds, and a peak inside its speech (0.25 s either side still inside). `paceFactorAfter(factor, words, peaks)` moves `factor` (`LIVE_PACE_FACTOR`, 0.69, when none is kept) halfway to such a take's own words over its peaks, kept within 0.45 to 1.1, to a thousandth, and gives any other take its factor back unchanged; a take that counts can give it back unmoved too, which is why a caller asks `countsForPaceFactor`. Pure: no DOM, store or Web Audio, and nothing is sent.
- `app.path` is the Loop Path (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-loop-path.md, which overrides docs/PLAN.md T42 and T43): four units of five levels, each level a loop of goal, take 1, review and take 2, which counts, beside check-backs, crowns, checkpoints and a benchmark. Pure: no DOM, store, clock or Math.random; a day is a local `"YYYY-MM-DD"` string the caller passes in (`localDay(value)` turns an ISO time or a Date into one, `addDays(day, days)` counts from it), and draws come only from the `random` passed in. `UNITS` holds `restarts`, `fillers`, `long-pauses` and `pace` in that order (the order of `backend/advice/select.py` MEASURED_SKILLS, which ties keep), each `{id, title, tags, subtitle, why, fixes, example, levels, crown, checkpoint}`: `why` is the unit's cited "why this works" line and each level's `tip` one research library entry's advice word for word, both `{entry, text, label, url}` with `label` built as backend `citation()` builds it; `creditOf(entryId) -> {short, work} | null` gives the same source's short credit, the surnames and year ("Pawlik and Perrin (2020)", "Bosker et al. (2013)" from four authors on, the publisher for a source with no author), and its work, the title (or the publisher without one), for the Path unit card's Source line (`frontend/tests/path_citations.test.js` checks them against `backend/advice/library.json`); `fixes` are the four fixes a speaker may choose for the next take and `example` the `{good, bad}` lines as parts `{text, mark}`. A level is `{id, unit, number, title, material, prepSeconds, takes, conditions, guards, scoredInPairs, tip, liveTap, spotIt, listsTopFillers, checkback}`, its id `"<unit>-<number>"` (the id `app.store` keeps once it is cleared), its `takes` `[{seconds, scored, read, band?, wpm?, label}]`, where a ladder read (Pace levels 1 and 2) has null `seconds` and `wpm` its target pace. A play is one thing started from the Path, its id the route id: a loop (`"fillers-3"`), a check-back (`"fillers-3_checkback"`), a crown (`"fillers_crown"`, `"pace_crown"`), a checkpoint (`"restarts_checkpoint"`) or `"benchmark"`. `levelById(id)` and `playById(playId, {baseline, cleared})` return null for an unknown id; a play is a frozen `{id, kind, unit, unitTitle, level, number, heading, subtitle, takes, material, prepSeconds, prepText, goal, example, why, tip, fixes, liveTap, spotIt, listsTopFillers, inRoomSwitch, tier}`, everything the Level screen shows and asks for, and `takeAt(play, takeIndex, {baseline, cleared, material})` resolves one take, a loop's retries past its list ("Try take 3") included: `material`, the passage drawn for the take, times a ladder read (its words at the read's target pace, rounded up), whose seconds are null without one. `measure(report, setSeconds)` reads one take's numbers from its `app.delivery` report as every rule reads them, `{spoke, setSeconds, speakingSeconds, minutes, fillers, fillersPerMinute, restarts, restartsPerMinute, shortMid, shortMidPerMinute, longPauses, longPausesPerMinute, wpm, cleanStretchSeconds, misreads, readSlips, followedScript, unreadRun, weightedPerMinute, otherPerMinute}`, rates and seconds to a tenth and wpm whole, as the screens show them, so a verdict never contradicts its copy; `spoke` is false for a take without speech, which fails every rule. `ownTakes(history)` leaves the sample out, as everything here does, and `baseline(history)` gives the speaker's usual numbers over their 5 latest own takes with speech, each rate their total count over their total minutes, or null with none; a play freezes it when its Goal step opens. `evaluate(play, takeIndex, report, {baseline, cleared, earlier, fix, inRoom}) -> {scored, passed, value, valueText, unitLabel, targetText, sentence, guards, fix, jumped, records, stage}` judges one take against its rule and its guards (`talkShare`, `minWpm`, `steadyOtherSlips` and `readThrough`, each `{id, passed, text, fix}`); the baseline may be null only on take 1, when the take itself stands in. `passed` is null on a take that is not scored, `sentence` the verdict line ("5.2 fillers a minute on take 2. The target was 6."), `fix` the one fix after a miss (the chosen fix, else the first failed guard's, else the level's first), `jumped` the later levels a passing loop take also clears, and `records` the ids `app.store.markLevelCleared` keeps on a pass (`"fillers-3"`, `"fillers-3_cb1"`, `"fillers_crown-2"`, `"fillers_checkpoint"` and `"fillers_checkpoint_room"`). `order(history)` returns the units weakest first: each unit's distance from target over the speaker's 5 latest takes with speech, each weighted by its speaking minutes, as select.py's `weakest_skills` ranks one take; ties and an empty history keep the order of `UNITS`. Each unit climbs on its own: level 1 is always open and level n + 1 opens once level n is cleared (`isUnlocked(level, cleared)`, `currentLevel(unit, cleared)`), and `statusOf(level, cleared, history, today) -> "cleared" | "current" | "locked" | "resting"` reads "resting" once `attemptsLeft(levelId, history, today)`, 3 scored attempts a local day, reaches 0. `ringOf(levelId, cleared) -> "none" | "half" | "gold"` follows the check-backs, and `checkbacks(cleared, history, today) -> [{levelId, stage, dueOn, due}]` lists every cleared level not yet gold: stage 1 due 2 days after the level was cleared, stage 2 7 days after or 2 days after stage 1's pass if that is later, a miss due again 2 days after it, and nothing ever locks; due ones come first. `crownState(unitId, cleared)` gives the optional crown that opens after level 3 (Fillers' sudden death, Pace's 180 wpm ladder; null for a unit without one), `checkpointState(unitId, cleared)` the checkpoint after level 5, `benchmarkState(history, today) -> {due, nextOn, sittings}` the benchmark, due with no sitting yet or 14 days after the latest, and `weekDays(history, today) -> {days, goal}` this week's days with a take, Monday to Sunday, against a goal of 4. Attempts, misses and sittings are read from saved takes' `path` field (the `app.store` session above). `materialFor(play, history, random, takeIndex)` gives what Practice's level mode shows, `{kind, id, title, text, targetWpm, topic}`, drawn once at the Goal step and kept for every take of a loop, except that each ladder (Pace) read draws its own cold read, over the run's own saved takes first; `pickTopic(history, promptId, random, exclude)` and `pickPassage(history, passages, random, exclude)` give a topic of the prompt or a passage of the list that no take has had, at random, else the one used longest ago, passing over `exclude` while another is left (Practice draws its Talk topics and Read passages with them), `pickColdRead(history, random)` is `pickPassage` over the cold reads, and `topFillers(history)` the speaker's 3 most used fillers as `[{text, count}]`.

`change`, the change line `{text, direction, trend}` or null, against the latest earlier own take that has the number (for reads, the latest earlier take that followed a script; for talks, the latest earlier take that did not), leaving out the take itself (`takeId`) and any take after `at` ("1.4 fewer than Tuesday", "Same as yesterday"; `direction` `"better"`, `"worse"` or `"same"` for the skill, `trend` `"down"`, `"up"` or `"flat"` for the number); `caught`, on a live-tap play, `{caught, total, text}` ("You caught 3 of 5 live."). `momentsFor(play, report, transcript, takeIndex)` gives up to three moments, earliest first, each `{kind, at, time, text, parts, flagged}` with `parts` the words around the slip as `[{text, marked}]`: each filler with 2 words either side (`"filler"`); or, for Pace, the take's thirds (`"pace"`: "0:00 to 0:30, 172 wpm", `flagged` outside the band). `matchTaps(taps, eventTimes, tapWindow) -> {caught, total, pairs, text}` matches each tap to at most one event not yet matched ("You caught 4 of 6."), with `SPOT_IT_WINDOW` (0.5 s before to 2 s after) for Spot it and `LIVE_TAP_WINDOW` (to 2.5 s after) for the live tap; Under 320px the body becomes one column with the ladder `--s-3` under the stats; under 200px the marks hide and the rows' padding and gap drop to `--s-1`.

**`.sr-only`**: the standard visually-hidden rule.

## 7. Recording states (Practice)

`<section class="screen" id="screen-practice" data-state="idle">` carries one of four values. CSS shows and hides children by `[data-state]`; `render(state)` sets the attribute and text. A normal take leaves the screen at Stop for a background job (the banner, section 6), so `uploading` and the Upload failed and Too short blocks after an upload are a level take's: the Level screen waits on it, and "Leave level" is the one way out.

| State | Timer | Record button | Tabs and chips | Below | Nav |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `idle` | `0:00`, muted (after a Stop under 5 s, that take's time until a new take starts or Practice is shown again); "/ 1:00" and an empty bar for timed options | mic, "Record" | enabled | hint "Tap to record. Stop uploads automatically." and a `.btn--ghost` "See a sample result" (section 3 covers short screens) | visible |
| `recording` | counting, text color, `.rec-dot`; the bar fills; under it the live pace row (section 6, Timer), never in level mode | stop, "Stop" | disabled; the selected chip stays readable; New topic hidden | hint "Tap Stop when you finish. It uploads right away."; the sample button takes no room; the text stays readable | hidden |
| `uploading` (level take) | final value held | hidden with its label | disabled | `.state--loading` "Analyzing your speech" / "Usually 5 to 15 seconds." No cancel; an upload still open after three minutes ends in the Network error. | hidden |
| `error` | hidden | hidden with its label | disabled | `.state--error` in the record zone with its title focused, fixed message, one action; after a failed upload (network, provider or unknown), a secondary "Record a new take" drops the take; a backend 400 drops it at once and shows Too short; under `max-height: 600px` the block takes the card's place | visible |

Mic permission is requested on the first record tap, never on load. Stopping under 5 s goes straight to `error`; nothing is uploaded. A level take the backend rejects as too short (400) is dropped and shows the same Too short block, since Retry would re-send a take that cannot pass; a normal take's 400 shows Too short in the banner. The blob stays in memory through `uploading` and `error` so Retry re-sends it. Reaching the time limit never stops a take. A take that is let go (saved, handed to a background job, dropped with "Record a new take" or by a backend 400, or left behind by leaving the screen) puts the clock back to 0:00, and the bar is empty in idle on every path. A recorder that cannot start (no MediaRecorder, or one that refuses the stream) starts no take: the microphone, the pause sampler and the live pace capture go at once, and the Microphone blocked block shows.

**Background jobs** (normal takes). At Stop the take goes to a job, Practice renders `idle` at 0:00 and the banner shows `working`. A saved take opens Results at once only while Practice is on show in `idle` in normal mode; one saved while a take records, an error block shows, a level is on, or another screen is open waits as `ready` for "See results", so nothing is pulled from under the speaker. The banner says one thing, in this order: the oldest failed job ("Upload failed", its fixed sentence, "Retry upload" and "Record a new take"), the oldest too-short one ("Too short", "Record again"), the ready ones ("Results ready" / "Your 0:42 take is ready to read."; "2 takes ready" / "See results opens the oldest first."), the working ones ("Analyzing your speech" / "Your 0:42 take. Usually 5 to 15 seconds."; "Analyzing 2 takes" / "Usually 5 to 15 seconds each."). "Retry upload" repeats only the step that failed, with the same blob and script; "Record a new take" and "Record again" drop the take and go to `#/practice`. The blob stays in memory while its job can retry, and is let go the moment the take is saved or the job is dropped. A take saved while the microphone prompt is open, the sample loads, or Results is already opening for an earlier take also waits as `ready`. After Stop the button reads Record in the same place, so record taps are ignored for 500 ms (a bounce or a double tap); a level take's button hides at Stop and needs no grace. Each new banner title goes through the live region ("Analyzing your speech", "Upload failed", "Too short"); "Results ready" is said when a take turns ready or opens Results from Practice, never twice for one take.

## 9. Accessibility

- Tap targets are at least 44 x 44px; interactive elements are real `<button>` or `<a>`.
- `:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 2px }` everywhere; never `outline: none` without a replacement. Scrollers and masked rows keep enough padding that a ring is never clipped; the nav items and segmented options, which touch an edge or a neighbor, draw it inside (`outline-offset: -3px`), in `--accent` on every option, the selected tint included. A masked scroller's ring is drawn by its container: while the passage text has keyboard focus the card draws it (without `:has`, the text keeps an inset ring).
- On Practice, History and Results a focused control scrolls clear of the nav and the action bar, ring and all, through scroll margins; the page gets no bottom scroll-padding, which would move it whenever Tab enters the Results bar. `html` carries `scroll-padding-top: var(--banner-h, 0px)`, the banner's height while it is up and 0 otherwise, so a control focus scrolls to clears the strip. History's open, trash, Yes and No buttons, and every button and `[tabindex]` node in Practice, carry `scroll-margin-top: var(--s-2)` and `scroll-margin-bottom: calc(var(--nav-h) + var(--s-2) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`. On Results every link or button above the stuck action bar carries `scroll-margin-bottom: calc(var(--nav-h) + var(--s-7) + var(--s-6) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`, and the bar's own button carries none; under `max-height: 480px` or `max-width: 319px`, where the bar sits at the end of the page, every link and button, the bar's own included, carries History's `calc(var(--nav-h) + var(--s-2) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`.
- The `.sr-only` region with `aria-live="polite"`, written only by `app.ui.announce`, says "Recording started", the elapsed time every 30 seconds, "Recording stopped, 42 seconds" ("1 second" for a one-second take), the new topic after each Talk chip or New topic tap, the passage's title after each Read chip or New passage tap, "Results ready" (once per take: as it turns ready in the banner, or as Results opens from Practice), the banner's other titles as they change ("Analyzing your speech", "Upload failed", "Too short"), "Session deleted" and the theme after each toggle. The banner itself is no live region. The live pace row is never announced: like the clock, it is `aria-live="off"`.
- Focus moves on purpose. After an in-app screen change it goes to the new screen's h1 (`tabindex="-1"`, focused with `preventScroll`), unless it already sits inside that screen or on the nav (a nav activation keeps focus on its link where the browser focuses clicked links, and the boot route moves nothing); a click or a tap shows no ring there, a keyboard activation the usual one. It goes to the error title (`tabindex="-1"`) when Practice shows an error. When focus was in the record zone, it goes back to the record button (the sample button after "Sample unavailable") when an error block's action returns to idle, and to the loading title (`tabindex="-1"`, never a Tab stop) when Stop or Retry upload starts a level take's upload in place, so "Analyzing your speech" is read; otherwise it stays where it is. Stop on a normal take keeps focus on the button, now Record. A banner repaint that drops the button focus sat on moves focus to the banner itself (`tabindex="-1"`, `preventScroll`) while the banner stays up; "See results" leaves it to the route, which focuses the Results h1. It moves to No when a delete confirm opens, back to the trash button on No, and after a delete to the row now in that place, or to the empty state's action when none is left. Every `focus()` call is guarded, because some test fakes have none; Practice also guards `contains()`, `querySelector` on its blocks and `document.activeElement`.
- Color never carries meaning alone: fillers are bold with labels, pause markers contain text, hints, status words and the live pace cue carry words and an icon, deltas carry a triangle and words, every speech-map mark sits beside a card that names its kind and detail, the chart has a table and its fillers band a legend. Every text pair is at least 4.5:1 and every meaningful non-text mark at least 3:1 in both schemes (section 4).
- Segmented controls are toggle buttons with `aria-pressed` inside `role="group"` with an `aria-label`.
- `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` stops the `.rec-dot` pulse and the spinner rotation; nothing else animates.
- Icons are inline SVG, `aria-hidden="true"`, beside visible text or an `aria-label`. One `h1` per screen, `h2` for sections. Tab order top to bottom, nav last.
- `lang="en"`, viewport `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`, no `user-scalable=no`. Layout survives 200% zoom.
