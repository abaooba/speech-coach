# Speech Coach frontend design spec

Every screen is built from this file. Angle: **drill-first**. A debater runs timed reps between rounds: one thumb, one glance, next rep. Numbers are big, chrome is minimal, nothing moves unless it carries information. Two agents building two screens without talking must produce siblings. If something is missing, use the closest existing token or class; never invent a parallel one or edit this file in a task.

## 1. Screens and navigation

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | `#/onboarding` | First launch only: what is measured, audio is deleted after analysis, one button. |
| Practice | `#/practice` (default) | Talk on a timed topic or read a passage aloud, record, stop. Home. |
| Results | `#/results/:id` | A two-sentence headline, four metric cards with "vs last take" lines, the speech timeline, the highlighted transcript, one or two tips. |
| History | `#/history` | Sessions newest first with a status word, tap to reopen, delete with confirm. |
| Progress | `#/progress` | Metric switch, summary row, hand-rolled SVG line chart with the target band and the personal best. |
| Path | `#/path` | The skill map: the four units weakest first, each a winding line of level nodes with its crown and checkpoint; the week and due check-back chips; the benchmark when due. No rank. |
| Level | `#/level/:playId` | One play step by step: Goal, takes through Practice's level mode, the review of take 1, Result. Its back link returns to the Path, whose nav item stays active. No rank. |
| Account | `#/account` | The optional account: sign up or log in; signed in, add this device's takes once, log out, or delete the account after a second tap. Linked from History's header; no nav item. |

**Flow.** On boot, if `app.store.getFlag("onboarded")` is false the router opens Onboarding; its single button sets the flag and goes to Practice. Practice is home: the empty hash, the left nav item, where "Record again" returns. It opens on the Talk tab with "Your day" selected; the tab, the option and the topic stay as the speaker left them while the app is open. Stop uploads immediately (no submit step); on success the session is saved and the router goes to `#/results/<id>`. Results keeps an action bar stuck above the nav with "History" (`.btn--ghost`) and "Record again" (`.btn--primary`), or at the end of the page on a short or zoomed window; History rows open Results by id. The nav shows Practice · Path · Progress · History; on Results no item is active, and on a level (`#/level/<playId>`) the Path item is. The nav is hidden on Onboarding and while the active Practice is `recording` or `uploading`, in level mode too, so a stray thumb cannot abandon a take.

**Path** (`#/path`, `app.screens.path`; docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-loop-path.md section 9). Under the h1, a row of status chips: "This week: 3 of 4 days" with the flame, plain text and never a button ("This week: 5 days, goal met" from 4 days), and, when check-backs are due, a button chip that starts the first one ("Due today: 1 check-back: Fillers 2", "Due today: 3 check-backs"). Then the benchmark: a card while one is due ("Benchmark day", "Same passage, same prompt, every 14 days. See your real progress.", "About 3 minutes", "Start benchmark"), otherwise, after a first sitting, one line ("Next benchmark: Tue, Oct 28") that from the second sitting adds the change since the first ("Since your first: read slips 6 → 2, fillers 5.1 → 2.4 a minute"). Then one block per unit in `app.path.order(history)` order: a unit card (h2 title, subtitle, the why line with its source link) over the unit's nodes as an ordered list, winding left and right of centre, with an SVG path behind them in `--ok` along cleared stretches and `--grid` elsewhere. A level node is cleared (a filled `--ok` circle with a check; a `--warn` arc over half its edge once check-back 1 passes, a full `--warn` ring, gold, "Locked in", once check-back 2 does; a small clock badge while a check-back is due), current (an `--accent` circle with the level number), locked (outlined, with a lock) or resting (outlined, with a moon, "Back tomorrow"). Beside level 3 sits the optional crown ("Sudden death, optional", "180 wpm ladder, optional"), locked until level 3 is cleared and filled `--warn` once earned; after level 5 the checkpoint flag ("Checkpoint: 2-minute new topic"). Every node is a button with an accessible name ("Fillers level 2, Spot it, cleared, check-back due") that opens an inline detail card: "Clear level 2 first." when locked; "Cleared Oct 14." then the due check-back with Start, its date, or "Locked in Oct 21." when cleared; Start for a crown or a checkpoint. The current node's card is open from the start, with its short goal and Start, and Escape closes a card and returns focus to its node. With no takes at all the Path still shows, every unit's level 1 current.

**Level** (`#/level/<playId>`, `app.screens.level`; the same spec, section 8). One play, step by step: a back link "Path" with a chevron-left icon to `#/path`, the h1 ("Fillers, level 3", "Fillers, level 3 check-back", "Fillers crown", "Fillers checkpoint" or "Benchmark") over the level title ("Catch it live"), and a stepper, an ordered list of `app.loop.stepsFor(play)` whose items are done (a check), current (`aria-current="step"`) or upcoming. Goal is a card with the target, large, with its unit label, the length, the example and the non-example and the unit's why line with its link (Fillers level 1 adds the speaker's top fillers, a Long pauses level with prep a "Start planning" countdown with Skip, a checkpoint the "Someone is listening (in the room)" switch); its primary button ("Record take 1", or "Read it" / "Record" on a one-take play) records through Practice's level mode (`startLevelTake`, section 2). After take 1 the Review step shows the "Take 1" feedback card (`app.loop.feedbackFor`: the number against its target, the change line, up to three moments, and on Fillers level 3 "You caught 3 of 5 live."), the level's tip card and "Review 3 moments (30 s)" ("Go to take 2" when there are no moments); then "Look at 3 moments", each moment with "Play 3 s" (one clip at a time, the button reading "Stop" while it plays) and "What happened?" chips (`aria-pressed`), Spot it first on Fillers level 2, the optional "Your fix for take 2" chips, the privacy line with a lock, "Take 1's audio stays in this phone's memory for this level only, then it is deleted. It is never saved.", and "Say it again" ("Read it again" on reads). The Result shows "Level cleared" with the verdict, the guard lines, any jump line, the two dated check-backs and "Start level 4" / "Back to path", or the value against the target, the failed guards and "The one fix: …" with "Try take 3" while attempts remain ("This level opens again tomorrow." when none do); a check-back, crown, checkpoint or benchmark shows its own verdict. Each step change is announced through `app.ui.announce` and moves focus to the new step's heading. The screen keeps one run in memory, `{playId, baseline, material, takes, choices, fix, audio}`, and releases take 1's audio (stops playback, closes the AudioContext, drops the buffer and the blob, revokes any object URL) when the Result shows, the speaker leaves the level, another play starts, or the router shows a screen other than Practice or this level. Playback decodes the blob on the first Play tap, a user gesture, with `AudioContext.decodeAudioData` and plays each clip with an `AudioBufferSourceNode` (`start(0, offset, 3)`), falling back to an `<audio>` element on an object URL; nothing plays on its own. A reload starts the level again at Goal, and without audio the Play buttons give way to "Take 1's audio is gone: it is kept only while this level is open." Neither the Path nor a level shows a rank, tier, rating or rank streak.

Routing is hash-based in `app.js`. Exactly one `.screen` has `.screen--active`; the others carry the `hidden` attribute. A route to another screen, or to another session on Results, scrolls the window to the top once the new section is active; a hash that lands on the page already shown keeps the scroll. The tab title is the screen's h1, then " · Speech Coach" ("Results · Speech Coach"); Onboarding, whose h1 is the app's name, keeps "Speech Coach".

## 2. File layout and the `app` namespace

```
frontend/
  index.html          eight <section class="screen">, <nav>, scripts in the order below,
                      one inline theme script in <head>
  styles.css          tokens, base, components, screens, one file
  app.js              creates window.app, hash router, boots on DOMContentLoaded
  ui.js               app.ui: el, renderState, clear, announce, formatClock, formatDate,
                      formatDay, icon, getTheme, applyTheme, setTheme, nextTheme
  auth.js             app.auth: the optional account (signUp, logIn, logOut,
                      deleteAccount, me) and the sync store.js connects to it
  store.js            app.store: IndexedDB sessions + flags + skill-path progress,
                      kept on the server too for a signed-in speaker
  api.js              app.api.analyze
  passages.js         app.passages: [{id, kind, title, text, targetWpm: [lo, hi], source?,
                      format?, timeLimitSeconds?, topics?}], app.passages.coldReads() and
                      app.passages.benchmark()
  delivery.js         app.delivery: the filler rules and the delivery report (analyze, classify)
  rank.js             app.rank, owner-written (T36): take scores, the rating and the tiers
  rankcard.js         app.rankCard: the rank card on Progress and Results (model, build); it
                      reads the rank only through app.rank (rank.js, owner-written, T36)
  path.js             app.path: the Loop Path's units, levels, plays and rules (measure,
                      baseline, evaluate, unlocking, check-backs, the benchmark, materials)
  loop.js             app.loop: the Level screen's pure helpers (steps, feedback, moments,
                      tap matching, clips, days in words)
  tips.js             app.tips.select(report, options) -> up to two {id, text}
  pauses.js           app.pauses, human-owned (T08); agents never touch it
  screens/practice.js results.js history.js progress.js path.js level.js account.js onboarding.js
  how.html            static "How we measure" page: each metric's rule, band and source
  sample-session.json one real take, loaded by Practice's "See a sample result"
  manifest.json  sw.js  icons/   (T10)
```

Script order: `app.js`, `ui.js`, `auth.js`, `store.js`, `api.js`, `passages.js`, `delivery.js`, `rank.js`, `rankcard.js`, `path.js`, `loop.js`, `tips.js`, `pauses.js`, then the eight screens. Classic `<script>` tags, no modules, no build, no CDN. The list is fixed by `backend/tests/test_frontend_shell.py`, so a new helper lives in the module that owns its data. A new file comes only when `docs/PLAN.md` names one, as it named `path.js`, and then joins this list, that test, `SHELL` in `sw.js` (with a `CACHE_NAME` bump) and `index.html` together. The Loop Path's scripts joined them that way (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-loop-path.md section 0). Every file is an IIFE assigning exactly one `app.<name>`; `window.app` is the only global. Each file opens with a two-to-four line comment saying what it owns. Transcript and user text never go through `innerHTML`; build nodes with `app.ui.el`, and SVG with `createElementNS`.

Contracts (exact names):

- `app.router.go(name, params)`, `app.router.current()`. A screen registers `app.screens[name] = { mount(root), show(params), hide() }`; `app.js` calls `mount` once at boot with its `<section id="screen-<name>" class="screen">`, then `show`/`hide` on each hash change. A module never touches DOM outside its `root`; shared data goes through `app.store` and route params.
- `app.store.save(session) -> Promise<id>`, `list()` newest first, `get(id)`, `remove(id)`, `getFlag(key)`, `setFlag(key, value)`. Session: `{id, createdAt, passageId, passageTitle, durationSeconds, metrics, transcript, topic, path}`; `metrics` is the backend dict unchanged, `transcript` the backend word list `[{text, start, end}]`, `topic` the Talk topic on screen when the take was recorded (null without one), and `path`, absent on a plain take, a Path take's `{play, kind, take, scored, passed, stage, inRoom}`: `kind` is `"loop"`, `"checkback"`, `"crown"`, `"checkpoint"` or `"benchmark"`, `passed` is null on a take that is not scored, `stage` is 1 or 2 on a check-back and `inRoom` a boolean on a checkpoint, each null otherwise. `topic` and `path` sync in the server report too. Audio is never stored: `save` throws on a session carrying `blob` or `audio`. Skill-path progress: `markLevelCleared(levelId, clearedAt?) -> Promise<{levelId, clearedAt}>` records an `app.path` level as cleared (`clearedAt` an ISO time, now unless given; a level cleared again keeps its first one) and `listClearedLevels()` lists the records in the order they were cleared. They live in the `pathProgress` store that database version 2 adds; the upgrade creates only that store, so saved sessions and flags stay. While a tab running an older version holds the database open, the upgrade waits for that tab to close, and every call fails at once with "Close other Speech Coach tabs to finish updating, then try again." rather than wait (boot then opens ungated); once the tab closes, the upgrade finishes and later calls go through.
- `app.passages`: `kind: "passage"` is read word for word and lists on the Read tab; `kind: "prompt"` is a topic to talk on and lists on the Talk tab. Every prompt has `timeLimitSeconds`. A prompt with `topics` shows one topic at a time, starting with the first, and New topic moves to the next one, wrapping; its `text` repeats `topics[0]` so every entry has text. The `free-talk` id is titled "Your day"; the id stays so saved takes still find their band. Prompts come first, because Practice opens on the first entry's tab with that entry selected. `app.passages.coldReads()` returns a new array of the cold reads for the skill path's cold-read mode: `kind: "passage"` entries with `coldRead: true` and a 140 to 160 wpm band, each 120 to 200 words of original debate-style argument with no digits, symbols or quotation marks and none of `app.delivery.HABIT_WORDS`, so a take read as written shows no misread and no filler. They are not entries of `app.passages`, so the Read tab never lists them. `app.passages.benchmark()` returns new `{read, talk}` objects on each call, the Path's benchmark (spec section 5): the passage `benchmark-read` ("Benchmark read", 140 to 180 words under the cold reads' rules) and the prompt `benchmark-talk` ("Benchmark talk", one topic in no other prompt, 90 s), both with a 140 to 160 band. Neither is in `app.passages` or `coldReads()`, so no other take practises them.
- `app.delivery.analyze(words, metrics) -> report` re-scores a stored take from its words, so every saved session uses today's rules: `kinds` (`"hesitation"`, `"habit"` or null per word), `fillers` (`{index, text, kind, start, end}`, "you know" is one event), `fillerCount`, `hesitationCount`, `habitCounts`, `topHabit` (`{text, count}` or null), `speechStart` (the first word's start, null without words), `speakingSeconds`, `wordsPerMinute` (hesitation sounds are not words), `fillersPerMinute`, `pauses` (`{at, length, midSentence, long}`; `long` marks a pause that reads 5.0 s or more, `LONG_PAUSE_SECONDS`, and falls between two words), `pauseCount` and `longestPause` (the pauses the Pauses card counts: every mid-sentence pause and every long one, even after a full stop), `midSentencePauseCount`, `longestMidSentencePause`, `longPauseCount`, `cleanStretch` (`{start, end, seconds}` or null), `repeatCount` and `restarts` (`{at, text, secondCopy}` per backend repeat, `secondCopy` the indexes of the words that say the phrase again), `followedScript` (true when `metrics` has a script block: the backend found the take follows the script it was sent; false for free talk and for a take that strays from its script, which the backend scores as free talk), `misreads` (`{at, word, said, wordIndex}` per `metrics.script.misread` entry, `wordIndex` the transcript word that said it, -1 when none does) and `skips` (`{at, word}` per `metrics.script.skipped` entry); both are empty for a take without a script block. Every time in the report is in recording seconds, the clock the tips print. A session with no words falls back to the backend tallies. Results, tips and Progress read this report; `metrics` stays the backend dict unchanged.
- `app.path` is the Loop Path (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-loop-path.md, which overrides docs/PLAN.md T42 and T43): four units of five levels, each level a loop of goal, take 1, review and take 2, which counts, beside check-backs, crowns, checkpoints and a benchmark. Pure: no DOM, store, clock or Math.random; a day is a local `"YYYY-MM-DD"` string the caller passes in (`localDay(value)` turns an ISO time or a Date into one, `addDays(day, days)` counts from it), and draws come only from the `random` passed in. `UNITS` holds `restarts`, `fillers`, `long-pauses` and `pace` in that order (the order of `backend/advice/select.py` MEASURED_SKILLS, which ties keep), each `{id, title, tags, subtitle, why, fixes, example, levels, crown, checkpoint}`: `why` is the unit's cited "why this works" line and each level's `tip` one research library entry's advice word for word, both `{entry, text, label, url}` with `label` built as backend `citation()` builds it (`frontend/tests/path_citations.test.js` checks them against `backend/advice/library.json`); `fixes` are the four fixes a speaker may choose for the next take and `example` the `{good, bad}` lines as parts `{text, mark}`. A level is `{id, unit, number, title, material, prepSeconds, takes, conditions, guards, scoredInPairs, tip, liveTap, spotIt, listsTopFillers, checkback}`, its id `"<unit>-<number>"` (the id `app.store` keeps once it is cleared), its `takes` `[{seconds, scored, read, band?, label}]`. A play is one thing started from the Path, its id the route id: a loop (`"fillers-3"`), a check-back (`"fillers-3_checkback"`), a crown (`"fillers_crown"`, `"pace_crown"`), a checkpoint (`"restarts_checkpoint"`) or `"benchmark"`. `levelById(id)` and `playById(playId, {baseline, cleared})` return null for an unknown id; a play is a frozen `{id, kind, unit, unitTitle, level, number, heading, subtitle, takes, material, prepSeconds, prepText, goal, example, why, tip, fixes, liveTap, spotIt, listsTopFillers, inRoomSwitch, tier}`, everything the Level screen shows and asks for, and `takeAt(play, takeIndex, options)` resolves one take, a loop's retries past its list ("Try take 3") included. `measure(report, setSeconds)` reads one take's numbers from its `app.delivery` report as every rule reads them, `{spoke, setSeconds, speakingSeconds, minutes, fillers, fillersPerMinute, restarts, restartsPerMinute, shortMid, shortMidPerMinute, longPauses, longPausesPerMinute, wpm, cleanStretchSeconds, misreads, readSlips, followedScript, unreadRun, weightedPerMinute, otherPerMinute}`, rates and seconds to a tenth and wpm whole, as the screens show them, so a verdict never contradicts its copy; `spoke` is false for a take without speech, which fails every rule. `ownTakes(history)` leaves the sample out, as everything here does, and `baseline(history)` gives the speaker's usual numbers over their 5 latest own takes with speech, each rate their total count over their total minutes, or null with none; a play freezes it when its Goal step opens. `evaluate(play, takeIndex, report, {baseline, cleared, earlier, fix, inRoom}) -> {scored, passed, value, valueText, unitLabel, targetText, sentence, guards, fix, jumped, records, stage}` judges one take against its rule and its guards (`talkShare`, `minWpm`, `steadyOtherSlips` and `readThrough`, each `{id, passed, text, fix}`); the baseline may be null only on take 1, when the take itself stands in. `passed` is null on a take that is not scored, `sentence` the verdict line ("5.2 fillers a minute on take 2. The target was 6."), `fix` the one fix after a miss (the chosen fix, else the first failed guard's, else the level's first), `jumped` the later levels a passing loop take also clears, and `records` the ids `app.store.markLevelCleared` keeps on a pass (`"fillers-3"`, `"fillers-3_cb1"`, `"fillers_crown-2"`, `"fillers_checkpoint"` and `"fillers_checkpoint_room"`). `order(history)` returns the units weakest first: each unit's distance from target over the speaker's 5 latest takes with speech, each weighted by its speaking minutes, as select.py's `weakest_skills` ranks one take; ties and an empty history keep the order of `UNITS`. Each unit climbs on its own: level 1 is always open and level n + 1 opens once level n is cleared (`isUnlocked(level, cleared)`, `currentLevel(unit, cleared)`), and `statusOf(level, cleared, history, today) -> "cleared" | "current" | "locked" | "resting"` reads "resting" once `attemptsLeft(levelId, history, today)`, 3 scored attempts a local day, reaches 0. `ringOf(levelId, cleared) -> "none" | "half" | "gold"` follows the check-backs, and `checkbacks(cleared, history, today) -> [{levelId, stage, dueOn, due}]` lists every cleared level not yet gold: stage 1 due 2 days after the level was cleared, stage 2 7 days after or 2 days after stage 1's pass if that is later, a miss due again 2 days after it, and nothing ever locks; due ones come first. `crownState(unitId, cleared)` gives the optional crown that opens after level 3 (Fillers' sudden death, Pace's 180 wpm ladder; null for a unit without one), `checkpointState(unitId, cleared)` the checkpoint after level 5, `benchmarkState(history, today) -> {due, nextOn, sittings}` the benchmark, due with no sitting yet or 14 days after the latest, and `weekDays(history, today) -> {days, goal}` this week's days with a take, Monday to Sunday, against a goal of 4. Attempts, misses and sittings are read from saved takes' `path` field (the `app.store` session above). `materialFor(play, history, random, takeIndex)` gives what Practice's level mode shows, `{kind, id, title, text, targetWpm, topic}`, drawn once at the Goal step and kept for every take of a loop; `pickTopic(history, promptId, random, exclude)` and `pickColdRead(history, random)` give a topic or a cold read no take has had, else the one used longest ago, and `topFillers(history)` the speaker's 3 most used fillers as `[{text, count}]`.
- `app.loop` (loop.js) holds the Level screen's pure helpers for one play, which read the rules only through `app.path` (no DOM, store, clock or Math.random). `stepsFor(play)` gives the stepper's steps `[{step, label, takeIndex}]`, `step` `"goal"`, `"take"`, `"review"`, `"result"`, `"intro"` or `"done"` and `takeIndex` the take a take step records (null otherwise): Goal · Take 1 · Review · Take 2 · Result for a loop (Goal · Read 1 · Review · Read 2 · Read 3 · Result with three reads), Goal · Take · Result for a check-back, crown or checkpoint, Intro · Read · Talk · Done for the benchmark. `feedbackFor(play, report, {takeIndex, baseline, cleared, earlier, history, takeId, at, today, liveTaps}) -> {value, valueText, unitLabel, targetText, change, caught}` is the feedback card after a take: the number, its words and its target from `app.path.evaluate`; `change`, the change line `{text, direction, trend}` or null, against the latest earlier own take that has the number (for a read, the latest that followed a script), leaving out the take itself (`takeId`) and any take after `at` ("1.4 fewer than Tuesday", "Same as yesterday"; `direction` `"better"`, `"worse"` or `"same"` for the skill, `trend` `"down"`, `"up"` or `"flat"` for the number); `caught`, on a live-tap play, `{caught, total, text}` ("You caught 3 of 5 live."). `momentsFor(play, report, transcript, takeIndex)` gives up to three moments, earliest first, each `{kind, at, time, text, parts, flagged}` with `parts` the words around the slip as `[{text, marked}]`: each filler with 2 words either side (`"filler"`); each restart with the words gone back over and the next 2 (`"restart"`) and each misread (`"misread"`); the long pauses, then the longest short mid-sentence ones (`"long-pause"`, `"pause"`: "we need to [5.2 s] the plan"); or, for Pace, the take's thirds (`"pace"`: "0:00 to 0:30, 172 wpm", `flagged` outside the band). `matchTaps(taps, eventTimes, tapWindow) -> {caught, total, pairs, text}` matches each tap to at most one event not yet matched ("You caught 4 of 6."), with `SPOT_IT_WINDOW` (0.5 s before to 2 s after) for Spot it and `LIVE_TAP_WINDOW` (to 2.5 s after) for the live tap; `fillerTimes(report)` are the events. `clipFor(momentOrSeconds) -> {offset, duration}` is what "Play 3 s" plays: from 1 s before the moment for 3 s, never before 0. `dateText(day, form)` writes a local day ("Friday, Oct 16" for `"long"`, "Fri, Oct 16" for `"short"`, else "Oct 16") and `whenText(day, today)` an earlier one ("earlier today", "yesterday", the weekday within 6 days, else "Oct 3"). `WHAT_HAPPENED` holds the review's four choices: "Lost my point", "Searching for a word", "Nerves", "Planned breath".
- `app.rankCard` (rankcard.js, docs/PLAN.md T37) is the rank card, drawn on Progress and Results only, never on the Path or in a level. `model(takes, options)` is pure (no clock, DOM or store) and reads the rank only through `app.rank` (`takeScore`, `rating`, `tier`, `streak`, `TIERS`; rank.js is owner-written), scoring each take through `app.screens.results.summarize` against its own band, as results.js `tierFor` does; samples and takes without a date never count. It returns null when rank.js or results.js is not loaded, with no rated take, or on Results for a sample or a take not in `takes`; otherwise `{tier, rating, floor, ceiling, fill, slices, ladder, next, change, notch, gate, foot}`. `options.screen` is `"progress"` or `"results"`: Progress passes `today` (a Date or an ISO time the streak counts to) and gets the streak foot; Results passes `takeId` and gets a card counted from the takes up to and including that one, so a reopened old take shows what it did then, with `change`, `notch` and the take's foot sentences. `build(model, {screen})` draws the card (section 6, Rank card) node by node with `app.ui.el` and the wedge with `createElementNS`; `screen` keeps the hidden heading's id unique (`rank-card-title-progress`, `rank-card-title-results`).
- `app.screens.results` also exports pure helpers that touch no DOM and no `app.ui`: `summarize(session)` (shared with History and Progress; its `timeUsed` is `{seconds, limitSeconds}` for a timed option, else null), `buildTranscriptPlan(words, pauses, restartWords, scriptSlips)` (a word whose index is in `restartWords` gets `restart: true`; with the report's `{misreads, skips}`, a misread word gets `misread`, the script word, and each skip is a `{type: "skip", text}` item, or for more than 8 in one place one `{type: "skip", text, count}` run), `headlineFor(summary, previousSummary) -> {strength, focus}`, `compareWithPrevious(summary, previousSummary) -> null | [four {direction, text} or null, in card order]` `timelineSegments(report) -> null | {start, seconds, fillers, pauses, restarts, cleanStretch}` and `isSample(session)`, true when `passageTitle` starts with "Sample: " (the bundled take "See a sample result" saves: History lists it, but no comparison and nothing on Progress uses it). `app.screens.history.statusFor(summary) -> {text, tone} | null`: null unless `summary.wpm` is above 0; pace is checked only against a two-number `summary.target`, both ends inside; fillers miss above 3 a minute; misses join with " · ", pace first; "On target" has tone `"ok"`, anything else `"warn"`. `app.screens.progress` exports `series` (samples left out), `scale` and `summaryFor(values, key, targets) -> {latest, best, bestIndex, average, change}`, which reads one metric's values oldest first with each take's `[lo, hi]` pace band; a null value marks a take with nothing measured, and `bestIndex` is -1 when no take can be best.
- `app.api.analyze(blob, contentType, script) -> Promise<{transcript, metrics}>`; `script` is the text the take was read from (a Read passage's text, or the script pasted on Talk), sent as the `script` form field when it holds more than spaces, so free talk leaves it out; the backend lines the take up against it and, when the take follows it, adds `metrics.script`. It rejects with an `Error` whose `.kind` is `"network" | "too_short" | "provider" | "unknown"` and whose `.message` is the matching string in section 8. A request still open 180 s after it starts (`ANALYZE_TIMEOUT_MS`, while sending or while reading the reply) is aborted through an `AbortController` and rejects as `"network"`; the backend gives the speech-to-text provider 150 s in all (`TRANSCRIBE_DEADLINE_SECONDS`), so its own `"provider"` error arrives first. Without `AbortController` or the timers there is no deadline.
- `app.ui.el(tag, attrs, children)`; `renderState(root, {kind, title, body, actionLabel, onAction, icon, secondaryLabel, onSecondary})`, kind `"empty" | "error" | "loading"`, icon `"mic" | "chart" | "search"`; `clear(root)`; `announce(text)` writes the `.sr-only` live region; `formatClock(seconds) -> "1:05"`; `formatDate(iso) -> "Oct 9, 10:21 PM"`; `formatDay(iso) -> "Oct 9"`; `icon(name, size = 20) -> <svg> | null` (null for an unknown name), a 24-unit icon in `currentColor`, `aria-hidden="true"`, `data-icon` set to its name, built on each call; the triangles and half of `contrast` are filled, the rest are 2-unit strokes: `mic`, `chart`, `search`, `alert`, `trash`, `check`, `arrow-right`, `refresh`, `triangle-up`, `triangle-down`, `dash`, `contrast`, `flame`, `moon`, `sun`, and for the Loop Path `route` (its nav item), `lock`, `flag`, `crown`, `clock`, `play`, `stop`, `lightbulb`, `external-link`, `chevron-left`, `calendar`, `close`, `user`. Theme: `getTheme() -> "auto" | "dark" | "light"` ("auto" when nothing is saved or storage is blocked), `applyTheme(choice)` sets `data-theme` on `<html>` (none for auto) and points both theme-color metas at that theme's `--bg`, `setTheme(choice)` saves then applies, `nextTheme(choice)` cycles auto, dark, light. The theme is the one setting kept in `localStorage` (key `speech-coach-theme`, every access in try/catch), because it is read synchronously; `app.js` applies it at boot, before any screen mounts. One inline script in `<head>`, right after the stylesheet and wrapped so it adds no global, sets `data-theme` from a saved "dark" or "light" before the first paint (Auto, an unknown value or blocked storage sets nothing); it has no `src`, so the fixed script list leaves it out. On a slow load only the theme-color metas follow the device scheme until `app.js` applies the theme at boot. Sessions and flags stay in IndexedDB.
- Practice keeps its state in one string, `app.screens.practice.state`; one `render(state)` function is the only code that touches that screen's DOM, apart from the clock and its bar (the timer paints them while a take runs, and the clock goes back to 0:00 when a take is let go), a focused chip scrolling into view, and the theme toggle, which repaints itself.
- `app.screens.practice.startLevelTake(request)` is the contract between the Level screen and Practice (spec section 7): it stores the request and goes to `#/practice`. The request is `{eyebrow, title, body, material, seconds, liveTap, judge, onSaved, onLeave}`. `eyebrow`, `title` and `body` fill the level banner (section 6): "Path: Fillers, level 3", "Take 1 of 2", "Talk for 90 s. Take 2 counts: 6 or fewer fillers a minute." `material` is `{kind: "prompt" | "passage", id, title, text, targetWpm, topic}`: `text` is what is shown and, for a passage, also the script sent to the backend; `topic` is the topic shown on a prompt, else null. `seconds` is the time limit, for "/ 1:30" and the limit bar. `liveTap` true shows the "Heard one" button while recording. `judge(result)`, with `result` `{transcript, metrics, durationSeconds}`, returns the session's `path`; `onSaved(saved)` gets `{id, session, blob, contentType, liveTaps}`; `onLeave()` runs when "Leave level" is tapped. While a request is held, Practice shows the banner; hides the mode switch, the chips, New topic, the script box and the sample button; shows the material's title, and its topic or passage text, in the passage card; and uses `seconds` as the limit. Record, Stop, too short, mic blocked, upload failed and retry behave exactly as in normal mode, and the nav hides while recording or uploading. On a successful upload Practice reconciles the metrics as usual, calls `request.judge(...)`, saves `{passageId: material.id, passageTitle: material.title, durationSeconds, metrics, transcript, topic: material.topic, path}`, clears the request and returns to normal mode, then calls `onSaved` with the blob and the live taps. It does not go to Results: the blob is handed over and Practice keeps no reference to it. Leave level clears the request, returns to normal idle and calls `onLeave()`. With `liveTap`, while recording, a large secondary button sits under the record button, "Heard one" (`aria-label="Heard one filler"`); each tap appends the elapsed seconds, to a tenth, and shows a small "3 heard" counter. Normal mode is unchanged apart from saving `topic` for every prompt with topics, and its blob is never kept or handed to anyone.

**Restart** `.tok--restart` (Results): each word of the second copy of a backend repeat (`metrics.repeats`: a phrase said twice, a restart after abandoned words, a cut-off restart such as "I kinda ner— I'm kinda nervous", or a script restart), as `report.restarts[].secondCopy` lists them: plain text with a 2px dotted underline in `--pause` offset by `--s-1`, `aria-label="and, restart"`; a filler inside the copy keeps its filler style and adds `.tok--restart`, its label ending ", restart" (`"so, verbal habit, restart"`). ## 7. Recording states (Practice)

A take that is let go (saved, dropped with "Record a new take" or by a backend 400, or left behind by leaving the screen) puts the clock back to 0:00, and the bar is empty in idle on every path.

## 9. Accessibility

- Tap targets are at least 44 x 44px; interactive elements are real `<button>` or `<a>`.
- `:focus-visible { outline: 3px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 2px }` everywhere; never `outline: none` without a replacement.
