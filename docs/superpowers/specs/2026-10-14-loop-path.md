## 1. Words used below

- **Unit**: `restarts`, `fillers`, `long-pauses` or `pace` (ids unchanged).
- **Level**: `<unit>-<n>`, n 1 to 5 (ids unchanged, so cleared levels already saved still count).
- **Play**: one thing started from the Path. Its id is the route id:
  - a level loop: `fillers-3`
  - a check-back of a cleared level: `fillers-3_checkback`
  - a crown: `fillers_crown`, `pace_crown`
  - a unit checkpoint: `restarts_checkpoint`, and the same for each unit
  - the benchmark: `benchmark`
- **Take**: one recording, numbered within its play run from 1.
- **Record ids**: kept with `app.store.markLevelCleared(id)` (pathProgress, synced for a
  signed-in speaker; every id matches the server's `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,100}$`):
  - `fillers-3`: the level is cleared.
  - `fillers-3_cb1`, `fillers-3_cb2`: check-back 1 or 2 passed.
  - `fillers_crown-1` to `fillers_crown-5`: the sudden death tiers.
  - `pace_crown-1`: the speed ladder crown.
  - `fillers_checkpoint`, and `fillers_checkpoint_room` when cleared in the room.
- **Today**: a local calendar day `"YYYY-MM-DD"`, passed into pure functions; they never read the
  clock. One helper turns an ISO time into its local day.

## 2. Measures, baseline and guards

A take's report is `app.delivery.analyze(transcript, metrics)`. From it and the take's **set
seconds** (its time limit):
- `minutes = speakingSeconds / 60`. A take without speech (`speechStart` null or
  `speakingSeconds` ≤ 0) fails every rule with "No speech was found in this take."
- `fillersPerMinute = fillerCount / minutes`
- `restartsPerMinute = repeatCount / minutes`
- `shortMid` counts pauses with `midSentence` true and `long` false;
  `shortMidPerMinute = shortMid / minutes`
- `longPauses = longPauseCount`
- `wpm = round(wordsPerMinute)`
- `cleanStretchSeconds` is `cleanStretch.seconds` rounded to a tenth, or 0
- `readSlips = repeatCount + misreads.length`. These are reading takes, scored only when the take
  followed its passage.
- `weightedPerMinute = (fillers×1 + restarts×1.5 + shortMid×1 + longPauses×3) / minutes`. These
  are rank.js's weights, without misreads and skips.
- `otherPerMinute(unit)` is the weighted rate with the unit's own skill left out:
  - fillers: no fillers
  - restarts: no restarts
  - long-pauses: neither pause kind (fillers×1 + restarts×1.5)
  - pace: nothing left out

Rounding: rates are compared and shown at one decimal, wpm whole, seconds to a tenth, so the copy
never contradicts the verdict ("6.0 a minute, target 6" passes).

**Baseline** (`app.path.baseline(history)`) comes from the 5 latest own takes with speech
(samples excluded, as `order()` excludes them). Each rate is the takes' total count over their
total minutes. wpm and clean stretch are minute-weighted means. It returns null with no takes.

A play freezes its baseline when its Goal step opens, from the history before the play's first
take. With a null baseline (a brand-new speaker), the play's first recorded take becomes the
baseline.

**Guards.** Every scored take must also pass the guards that apply to it. Each guard yields a
line with a pass or fail state and this copy:
- `talkShare` (talk takes): `speakingSeconds ≥ 0.8 × setSeconds`.
  - Pass: "Talked 88 s of 90".
  - Fail: "You talked 50 s of 90. Keep going to at least 72 s."
- `minWpm` (talk takes outside the Pace unit, and Restarts reads): `wpm ≥ 120`.
  - Pass: "142 wpm".
  - Fail: "104 wpm. Keep a pace of at least 120."
- `steadyOtherSlips` (every scored take except the benchmark):
  `otherPerMinute ≤ baseline.otherPerMinute + 1.0`.
  - Pass: "Other slips steady".
  - Fail: "Other slips rose to 4.1 a minute from 2.0. Keep them within 1 of your usual."
- `readThrough` (reading takes): `followedScript` is true, and no more than 8 script words are
  skipped in one place. On a fail, path.js's existing NOT_FOLLOWED and notReadThrough sentences
  are the copy.

## 3. The levels

Material:
- `your-day`: the `free-talk` prompt, with a topic picked by `pickTopic`.
- `impromptu`: the `impromptu` prompt, with a picked topic.
- `new-topic`: an impromptu topic no take has used (`topic` field). Once all are used, the least
  recently used one.
- `cold-read`: an unseen cold read (`pickColdRead`).
- `ladder`: the `explainer-rainbows` passage ("Explainer: how rainbows form", 109 words). It
  replaced the `rebuttal-uniforms` passage on 2026-10-14 (everyday fluency, not debate) at the
  same length, so every ladder read keeps its set seconds.

A loop uses the same material for every take. A check-back, checkpoint or crown always uses new
material.

Read set seconds: 90 for cold reads (as today). For the ladder, the passage's words at the take's
target wpm, rounded up to whole seconds.

Each level also has:
- a goal headline and unit label ("6 or fewer", "fillers a minute")
- a length ("90 s, two takes")
- a short goal for the Path popover ("6 or fewer /min")
- one example line and one non-example line
- one cited tip
- four fixes for take 2

The 2-take talk levels have takes 1 and 2, and take 2 is scored.

### Fillers (talk; guards talkShare, minWpm, steadyOtherSlips)

| n | title | material | takes (s) | scored rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name it | your-day | 60, 60 | fillers/min ≤ T, where T = round1((b + 8) / 2) if baseline b > 8, else 8 |
| 2 | Spot it | your-day | 60, 60 | ≤ 8 |
| 3 | Catch it live | impromptu | 90, 90 | ≤ 6 |
| 4 | Pause instead | impromptu | 90, 90 | ≤ 5 |
| 5 | New topic | new-topic | 120, 120 | ≤ 3 |

- **Level 1**: the Goal step lists the speaker's top 3 fillers (`app.path.topFillers(history)`:
  filler texts, lowercased, over the 5 latest own takes, by count, ties by first seen). With no
  fillers counted yet it says "No fillers counted yet. Take 1 will find yours."
- **Level 2**: the Review step starts with Spot it (section 8).
- **Level 3**: Practice shows the live "Heard one" button during both takes.
- **Check-back**: one take on a new topic, 60 s for levels 1 and 2 and 90 s for levels 3 to 5.
  Rule: the level's rate (8 for level 1), plus the guards.
- **Checkpoint**: 120 s, new topic. It passes when fillers/min ≤ 3 and
  `weightedPerMinute ≤ baseline.weightedPerMinute`, plus talkShare and minWpm.
- **Crown** (opens once level 3 is cleared): Sudden death on your-day, with the existing
  suddenDeath judge. The tiers are 30, 45, 60, 90 and 120 s. Set seconds are the tier plus 15. The
  next tier is the first one not yet earned.
- **Fixes**: "Pause instead of um", "Jot 3 points first", "Slow the first 3 words", "Finish the
  sentence, don't restart".
- **Example**:
  - ✓ "Close your lips and pause. The plan costs less."
  - ✗ "Um, so, the plan, uh, costs less." (the ums marked as fillers)

### Restarts (reads: guards readThrough, minWpm, steadyOtherSlips; level 5: talk guards)

| n | title | material | takes | scored rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read it 3 times | cold-read | read 1, review, reads 2 and 3 | read 3: readSlips ≤ 4 |
| 2 | Read it 3 times | cold-read (new) | same | read 3: ≤ 3 |
| 3 | Read it twice | cold-read (new) | read 1, review, read 2 | read 2: ≤ 2 |
| 4 | Read it twice | cold-read (new) | same | read 2: ≤ 1 |
| 5 | Say it twice | impromptu | 90, 90 | take 2: restarts/min ≤ 1 |

- **Moments after read 1**: each restart with the words gone back over, and each misread (the
  said word against the script word).
- **Check-backs**:
  - Levels 1 to 4: one unseen cold read at the level's slip limit.
  - Level 5: 90 s on a new topic, at most 1 restart a minute.
- **Checkpoint**: 120 s on a new topic. It passes at restarts/min ≤ 1 with `weightedPerMinute` at
  or below baseline. No crown.
- **Fixes**: "Finish the sentence, don't restart", "Read a phrase ahead", "Slow the first 3
  words", "Pause at commas".
- **Example**:
  - ✓ "I'm kinda nervous, but here's my point."
  - ✗ "I kinda ner— I'm kinda nervous." (the restart marked)

### Long pauses (talk, impromptu, same topic for both takes; guards talkShare, minWpm, steadyOtherSlips with fillers + restarts)

| n | title | prep | takes (s) | take 2 rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hold 15 s clean | 30 | 90, 90 | long pauses ≤ 1, clean stretch ≥ 15 |
| 2 | Hold 20 s | 20 | 90, 75 | no long pause, clean ≥ 20 |
| 3 | Hold 30 s | 10 | 90, 60 | no long pause, clean ≥ 30 |
| 4 | Hold 45 s | 0 | 120, 90 | no long pause, shortMid/min ≤ baseline's, clean ≥ 45 |
| 5 | Hold a minute | 0 (new topic) | 120, 120 | no long pause, clean ≥ 60 |

- **Prep**: shown on the Goal step before take 1 only. "Plan for 30 s: jot 3 points."
- **Check-back**: 90 s on a new topic with no prep. It needs the level's long-pause limit and its
  clean stretch.
- **Checkpoint**: 120 s on a new topic, with no long pause and weighted slips at or below
  baseline.
- **Fixes**: "Jot 3 points first", "Say your next point out loud", "Pause at a full stop, not
  mid-sentence", "Slow the first 3 words".
- **Example**:
  - ✓ "Uniforms cost more. Here's why: families still buy weekend clothes."
  - ✗ "Uniforms cost more because… [6 s] …families still buy clothes." (the pause marked)

### Pace (band = the material's `targetWpm`; ladder 150 ± 10 unless set below)

| n | title | material | takes | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Feel the band | ladder | reads at 150 ± 10, twice | read 2: wpm in 140 to 160 (readThrough) |
| 2 | Shift gears | ladder | read 1 at 140 ± 10, read 2 at 160 ± 10 | both reads scored. Each in its band with restarts ≤ 2 (readThrough). Both must land. |
| 3 | Talk in the band | impromptu | 90, then 70 if baseline wpm < band low, else 90 | take 2 wpm in band (talkShare, steadyOtherSlips) |
| 4 | Hold it | impromptu | 120, 120 | take 2 in band (same guards) |
| 5 | New topic | new-topic | 120, 120 | take 2 in band, and `weightedPerMinute ≤ baseline.weightedPerMinute` (talkShare) |

- **Check-back**: 90 s on a new topic, in the band.
- **Checkpoint**: 120 s on a new topic, in the band, with weighted slips at or below baseline.
- **Crown** (opens once level 3 is cleared): the speed ladder at 180 ± 10 wpm with at most 1
  restart on the ladder passage, using the existing speedLadder judge. One tier.
- **Fixes**: "Match the time bar", "Breathe at each full stop", "Pause at commas, not
  mid-phrase", "Keep going through small slips".
- **Example**:
  - ✓ "A steady 150: every word lands, and the pauses fall at full stops."
  - ✗ "A rushed 190 that runs one sentence into the next."

### Unit cards and tips

Each unit has a "why this works" line plus a citation.
- **Restarts**: "Restarts fade last: in one study they dropped only after the 4th repeat."
  (library restart-021)
- **Fillers**: noticing your own ums and pausing instead cut fillers for nearly every speaker in
  small studies (filler-087 or filler-085). Write the sentence so it says only what the entry
  says.
- **Long pauses**: retelling the same talk in less time cut pauses and carried over to a new
  topic (general-014).
- **Pace**: listeners judge fluency mostly by speed and how often you pause (pause-029).

Each level's tip is one entry from `backend/advice/library.json` with a matching tag:
- Show the entry's `advice` word for word.
- The citation label is built the way `backend/advice/__init__.py citation()` builds it.
- The link is the entry's `source.url`. It opens in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`.

A new node test, `path_citations.test.js`, reads library.json. It checks every tip and why line
against it: exact advice text, URL and label for tips; the URL and an entry id for why lines.

## 4. Unlocking, jumps and rest days

- Each unit climbs on its own:
  - Level 1 is always open.
  - Level n + 1 opens once record `<unit>-<n>` exists.
  - The crown opens after level 3, and the checkpoint after level 5.
- **Rest until tomorrow**: a loop allows 3 scored attempts per local day:
  - the first scored take
  - "Try take 3"
  - "Try take 4"

  For Pace level 2 an attempt is a pair of reads. After 3 misses today, `statusOf` gives
  `"resting"` and the level reads "Back tomorrow". Attempts are counted from saved takes
  (`path.kind === "loop"`, same play, `scored`, `passed === false`, today).
- **Jump test**: when a scored take passes level N, each later level M of that unit, in order, is
  cleared too while all of these hold:
  - M has a single scored take.
  - M's kind (talk or read) matches N's.
  - The take's set seconds are at least M's scored take seconds.
  - The take meets M's rule and guards against the same baseline.

  The first M that fails stops the jump. New-material requirements are ignored for a jump.
  Jump-cleared levels get the same clearedAt and their own check-backs. The Result says "Your take
  also cleared levels 4 and 5."
- `statusOf(level, cleared, history, today)` returns `"cleared" | "current" | "locked" |
  "resting"`.
- `ringOf(levelId, cleared)` returns `"none" | "half" | "gold"`.

## 5. Check-backs, crowns, checkpoints, the benchmark, the week

**Check-backs** for a cleared level L, cleared on local day c:
- Stage 1 is due on c + 2.
- Stage 2 opens once stage 1 passes. It is due on c + 7, or 2 days after stage 1's pass,
  whichever is later.
- A missed stage is due again 2 days after the miss. Nothing locks.
- A pass records `<L>_cb1` (the node gets a half ring) or `<L>_cb2` (the node turns gold,
  "Locked in").
- `app.path.checkbacks(cleared, history, today)` returns `[{levelId, stage, dueOn, due}]`:
  - It lists every level not yet gold.
  - Due ones come first, by date, then by unit order.
  - Misses are read from saved takes (`path.kind === "checkback"`, `passed === false`).

**Crowns** are optional nodes beside level 3. They are never required, and have no daily limit.
They are one-take plays: Goal, Take, Result.

**Checkpoints** are flag nodes after level 5, also one-take plays:
- The Goal step has a switch, "Someone is listening (in the room)".
- A pass records `<unit>_checkpoint`. With the switch on it also records
  `<unit>_checkpoint_room`, and the Result says "Cleared in the room".

**Benchmark**: one sitting of two takes:
1. The benchmark read: a held-out passage.
2. The benchmark talk: a held-out prompt, 90 s.

`app.passages.benchmark()` returns `{read, talk}`:
- `read`: kind "passage", id "benchmark-read", title "Benchmark read".
  - Its text is original, debate-style and 140 to 180 words.
  - It has no digits, symbols, quotation marks or habit words.
  - It shares no sentence with any other passage.
  - targetWpm is [140, 160].
- `talk`: kind "prompt", id "benchmark-talk", title "Benchmark talk".
  - One topic that is in no other prompt.
  - timeLimitSeconds 90, targetWpm [140, 160].
- Neither is listed in `app.passages` or in `coldReads()`.

When the benchmark is due:
- There are no benchmark takes yet, or the latest sitting's day is at least 14 days before today.

`app.path.benchmarkState(history, today)` returns
`{due, nextOn, sittings: [{on, readSlips, readWpm, fillersPerMinute, weightedPerMinute, wpm}]}`.
A benchmark never clears or locks anything.

**This week**: `app.path.weekDays(history, today)` returns `{days, goal: 4}`, where days is the
number of local days, Monday to Sunday of today's week, with at least one own take.

## 7. Practice level mode (the contract between screens/level.js and screens/practice.js)

`app.screens.practice.startLevelTake(request)` stores the request and goes to `#/practice`. The
request is:

```
{
  eyebrow: "Path: Fillers, level 3",          // banner line 1
  title: "Take 1 of 2",                        // banner title
  body: "Talk for 90 s. Take 2 counts: 6 or fewer fillers a minute.",
  material: { kind: "prompt" | "passage", id, title, text, targetWpm, topic },
  // topic: the topic shown on a prompt, else null.
  // text: what is shown. For a passage it is also the script sent to the backend.
  seconds: 90,                                 // the time limit: "/ 1:30" and the limit bar
  liveTap: false,                              // true: the "Heard one" button while recording
  judge(result),   // result = {transcript, metrics, durationSeconds}; returns the session's `path`
  onSaved(saved),  // saved = {id, session, blob, contentType, liveTaps}
  onLeave(),       // "Leave level" tapped
}
```

While a request is held, Practice:
- Shows the banner (DESIGN.md "Level banner": eyebrow, title, body, and a "Leave level" text
  button).
- Hides the mode switch, the chips, New topic, the script box and the sample button.
- Shows the material's title, and its topic or passage text, in the passage card.
- Uses `seconds` as the limit.

Record, Stop, too short, mic blocked, upload failed and retry all behave exactly as today.

On a successful upload, Practice:
1. Reconciles the metrics as today.
2. Calls `request.judge(...)`.
3. Saves `{passageId: material.id, passageTitle: material.title, durationSeconds, metrics,
   transcript, topic: material.topic, path}`.
4. Clears the request and returns to normal mode.
5. Calls `onSaved` with the blob and the live taps.

It does not go to Results. The blob is handed over and Practice keeps no reference to it.

- **Normal mode**: unchanged, apart from saving `topic`. The blob is never kept or handed to
  anyone.
- **Leave level**: clears the request, returns to normal idle, and calls `onLeave()`.
- **Live tap**: while recording with `liveTap`, a large secondary button sits under the record
  button.
  - Its label is "Heard one", its aria-label "Heard one filler" (the name starts with the visible
    label, WCAG 2.5.3).
  - Each tap appends the elapsed seconds, to a tenth, and shows a small "3 heard" counter.
- The nav hides while recording or uploading, as today.

## 8. Level screen `#/level/<playId>`

**Header**:
- A back link "Path" with a chevron-left icon, to `#/path`.
- The h1:
  - a loop: "Fillers, level 3"
  - a check-back: "Fillers, level 3 check-back"
  - "Fillers crown", "Fillers checkpoint" or "Benchmark" for the other plays.
- A subtitle with the level title ("Catch it live").
- On `#/level` the nav's Path item is the active one.

**Stepper**: an `ol`, with each item done (check), current (`aria-current="step"`) or upcoming.
The steps depend on the play:

| Play | Steps |
|---|---|
| Loop with 2 takes | Goal · Take 1 · Review · Take 2 · Result |
| Loop with 3 reads | Goal · Read 1 · Review · Read 2 · Read 3 · Result |
| Pace level 2 | Goal · Read 1 · Review · Read 2 · Result |
| Check-back, crown or checkpoint | Goal · Take · Result |
| Benchmark | Intro · Read · Talk · Done |

**The run.** The screen keeps one run in memory:
`{playId, baseline, material, takes[], choices, fix, audio}`.

The run ends, and its audio is released, when any of these happens:
- the Result shows
- the speaker leaves the level
- another play starts
- the router shows a screen other than Practice or this level

Released means: stop playback, close the AudioContext, drop the buffer, revoke any object URL,
and drop the blob.

Showing the screen with no run for that play starts at Goal. After a reload, a level always
starts again at Goal.

**Goal**:
- A card with:
  - the headline target, large, with its unit label
  - the length
  - the example (✓) and the non-example (✗)
  - the unit's why line with its citation link
- Fillers level 1 adds its top fillers. Long-pause prep adds a "Start planning" countdown (with a
  "Skip"), announced at its start and end.
- A checkpoint adds the in-the-room switch.
- The primary button is "Record take 1", or "Read it" / "Record" on one-take plays. It calls
  `startLevelTake`.

**Feedback** (after take 1, inside the Review step):
- A "Take 1" card holds:
  - The big value with its unit label and "target 6".
  - The change line against the speaker's latest earlier own take that has this value (for reads,
    the latest earlier take that followed a script).
    - Better: "1.4 fewer than Tuesday", in `--ok` with triangle-down. Worse: muted. The same:
      "Same as Tuesday".
    - When: "earlier today", "yesterday", the weekday within 6 days, else "Oct 3".
  - "Three moments to look at": up to 3 moments, each "0:12 so the, **um**, the plan costs".
  - Fillers level 3 adds "You caught 3 of 5 live."
- Then the level's tip card, then "Review 3 moments (30 s)", or "Go to take 2" when there are no
  moments.

`app.loop.momentsFor(level, report)` returns up to 3 moments, earliest first:

| Unit | Moments |
|---|---|
| Fillers | Each filler, with 2 words before and 2 after |
| Restarts | Each restart: its text plus the next 2 words. Each misread: the said word against the script word |
| Long pauses | Long pauses first, then the longest short mid-sentence pauses, written "we need to [5.2 s] the plan" |
| Pace | The take's thirds, each "0:00 to 0:30, 172 wpm", flagged when outside the band |

**Review**:
- The title is "Look at 3 moments", with "about 30 s" beside it.
- Each moment card holds:
  - the time and the words
  - "Play 3 s", which plays take 1's audio from 1 s before the moment for 3 s. Only one plays at
    a time; the button reads "Stop" while playing.
  - "What happened?": single-choice chips with `aria-pressed`: "Lost my point", "Searching for a
    word", "Nerves", "Planned breath".
- Fillers level 2 adds **Spot it** first: "Play take 1", which plays the whole take, and a big
  "Heard one" button that is live while it plays. At the end it shows "You caught 4 of 6."
  - `app.loop.matchTaps(taps, eventTimes, {before: 0.5, after: 2.0})` matches each tap to at most
    one unmatched event.
  - Live taps use `{before: 0.5, after: 2.5}`.
- "Your fix for take 2": the level's 4 fixes as single-choice chips. Choosing one is optional.
- The privacy line, with a lock icon: "Take 1's audio stays in this phone's memory for this level
  only, then it is deleted. It is never saved."
- The primary button is "Say it again", or "Read it again" on reads.
- Without audio (the page was reloaded), the Play buttons give way to "Take 1's audio is gone: it
  is kept only while this level is open."

**Playback**:
- On the first Play tap (a user gesture), decode the blob with `AudioContext.decodeAudioData`.
  Then play clips with an `AudioBufferSourceNode`: `start(0, offset, 3)`.
- If decoding fails, fall back to an `<audio>` element on an object URL.
- Nothing plays on its own.

**Take 2 and later**: Practice again. The banner body names the fix, if one was chosen: "Your
fix: Pause instead of um. Take 2 counts: 6 or fewer fillers a minute."

**Result** (`app.path` judges; this screen records):

*Pass*:
- A green card: "Level cleared", then "5.2 fillers a minute on take 2. The target was 6.", then
  the guard lines.
- The jump line, if any.
- A "Check back" card: "A level counts when it sticks. One quick take on a new topic:", then two
  dated rows, "Thursday, Oct 16: check-back 1" and "Tuesday, Oct 21: check-back 2, turns the node
  gold".

- Records go through `markLevelCleared` (the level and any jumped levels).

*Not yet*:
- The value against the target, then the failed guards, then "The one fix: …". That is the

- "Try take 3" or "Try take 4" while attempts remain today, then "Back to path".

*Check-back*: one of these, with the record when passed:
- "Check-back passed: half ring"
- "Locked in: the node turns gold"
- "Missed. It comes back on Thursday, Oct 16. Nothing locks."

*Crown and checkpoint*: their verdict line, plus the record.

*Benchmark Done*: this sitting's numbers, and the change since the first sitting when there is
one.

Announce each step change through `app.ui.announce`. Keyboard focus goes to the new step's
heading. No rank, tier or rating appears on this screen.

## 9. Path screen `#/path`

- h1 "Path". Under it, a row of status chips:
  - "This week: 3 of 4 days" with a flame icon. At 4 or more: "This week: 5 days, goal met". It
    is plain text, not a button.
  - When check-backs are due, a button chip: "Due today: 1 check-back: Fillers 2", or "Due today:
    3 check-backs". It starts the first due check-back.
- The benchmark:
  - When due, a card: "Benchmark day", "Same passage, same prompt, every 14 days. See your real
    progress.", "About 3 minutes", and "Start benchmark".
  - Otherwise, after at least one sitting, one line: "Next benchmark: Tue, Oct 28". With 2 or more
    sittings, add "Since your first: read slips 6 → 2, fillers 5.1 → 2.4 a minute".
- One block per unit, in `app.path.order(history)` order:
  - A unit card: h2 title, subtitle, and the why line with its link.
  - The unit's nodes as an ordered list, in the winding layout of the design.
  - Node positions alternate left and right of centre. An SVG path behind the nodes joins them:
    cleared stretches in `--ok`, the rest in `--grid`.
- Node states:
  - **cleared**: a filled `--ok` circle with a check. The half ring is a `--warn` arc over half the
    edge; gold is a full `--warn` ring. A due check-back adds a small clock badge.
  - **current**: an `--accent` circle with the level number. Beside it, an inline card with the
    short goal and a "Start" button.
  - **locked**: an outlined circle with a lock.
  - **resting**: an outlined circle with a moon, labeled "Back tomorrow".
  - **crown**: a small node with a crown icon beside level 3. Its label is "Sudden death,
    optional" or "180 wpm ladder, optional", locked until level 3 is cleared. When earned, the
    crown is filled `--warn`.
  - **checkpoint**: a flag node after level 5, labeled "Checkpoint: 2-minute new topic".
- Every node is a button with an accessible name, such as "Fillers level 2, Spot it, cleared,
  check-back due". Tapping one opens an inline detail card:
  - locked: "Clear level 2 first."
  - cleared: "Cleared Oct 14.", then "Check-back 1 is due today" with a Start button, or the next
    date, or "Locked in Oct 21."
  - crown and checkpoint: Start buttons.
- The current node's card is open from the start. Escape closes an open card and returns focus
  to its node.
- No rank, tier, rating or rank streak appears on this screen.
- With no takes at all, the Path still shows. Every unit's level 1 is current.
