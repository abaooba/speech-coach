purpose, with what it pinned before and what it pins now. A test is never skipped, deleted to get
green or weakened: each new assertion pins the new rule as strictly as the old one pinned the old.
The last column names the section of the design spec; "10-18" points into
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-fresh-reads-and-rehearsal-design.md, which section 4 builds.

## Amendment 2026-10-24: rough pace before tuning (section 7)

The owner chose option C on 2026-10-24: a take whose mode has no kept factor shows a rough number
with no cue, instead of the "Live pace starts after a 30 s talk." or "... read." sentence (design
spec section 7, "Rough pace before tuning (2026-10-24)"). These tests were on main at 2d6d8b4.
The new behavior also has a new file, `frontend/tests/practice_live_pace_rough.test.js`. In the
rows, roughRow(N) is the row exactly: parts ["span.practice__pace-number num", '" "',
"span.practice__pace-rough"] and text "About N wpm Rough until tuned"; sentenceRow(S) is the row
exactly: parts ["span.practice__pace-placeholder"] and text S.
